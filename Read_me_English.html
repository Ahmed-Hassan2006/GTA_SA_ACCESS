GTA: San Andreas Access Project — The Largest Accessibility Layer for the Blind

Designed & Developed by: Ahmed Hassan Abdelnabi

Current Version: 3.4

Peace be upon you (As-salamu alaykum),

Greetings to all my friends and brothers everywhere,


Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas is one of the greatest open-world games in video game history, and for many years it was a dream and passion for many blind gamers, including myself. Many believed that experiencing a 3D game of this scale and complexity without sight was nearly impossible.

Today, I share with you the result of a long, challenging journey of research, software modification, and continuous development to turn that dream into an enjoyable reality through the accessibility layer: GTA: San Andreas Access.

[1] The Software Development Journey & How the Project Started:

I began my journey by researching the structure of the GTA game engine and exploring ways to modify it and connect it to screen readers using scripts, C++, and direct memory manipulation (Memory Hooks).

  • In the Beginning: My goal was very simple and limited—just getting the screen reader to speak the main menu text.
  • Expansion & Development: After succeeding in the first step, I decided not to stop. I gradually expanded the project step by step to include environment scanning, object tracking, combat, navigation, and maps, eventually reaching integrated systems for a shop, inventory, leveling, and advanced audio/command systems.
  • Programming References & Documentation: Through personal research and continuous study, I accessed open-source programming repositories dedicated to documenting GTA opcodes and scripting commands on GitHub and specialized technical websites, which served as a primary guide for understanding the internal mechanics of the engine.
  • Role of Artificial Intelligence: I utilized AI to analyze some in-game images and screenshots to clarify visual details and assist with logical brainstorming to overcome technical obstacles.

[2] Access Versioning System:

The Access layer versioning follows a two-number format separated by a dot (e.g., 1.8 or 3.4):

  • First Number (on the left): Indicates the major public version officially released and shared with general users.
  • Second Number (on the right): Indicates the number of internal beta builds and test versions I tested and refined before reaching this stable release.
  • Example: Version 3.4 means this is the third major public release, preceded by 4 beta iterations and major improvements following version 2.6.

Current Version: 3.4

[3] The Full Localization Epic & The "Text Dictionary" Concept:

To deliver an authentic, complete Arabic experience alongside English that matches the original game text:

  1. I unpacked the game's core text file known as american.gxt.
  2. I extracted the texts, dialogues, and missions to translate them accurately.
  3. I programmed a system inside the accessibility layer based on a Dictionary Lookup System, which intercepts in-game String IDs and replaces them on the fly with spoken Arabic translations via the screen reader—making the game's story, dialogues, and menus (including train stations and items) fully supported in both Arabic and English.

[4] Vehicle Driving, Enhanced Ghost Mode, Aircraft & Trains:

During development, I worked hard to build an audio vehicle navigation system based on reading the game's Path Nodes network, but I encountered severe technical difficulties; accurately tracking complex road lanes and turns in real-time via audio while avoiding obstacles and sudden traffic is extremely complex for an accessibility layer.

  • Vehicle Ghost Mode (Alt + G): Allows your vehicle to pass smoothly through obstacles and objects without colliding with them.
  • Ghost Mode Upgrade Updated in 3.4: Whereas Ghost Mode used to stop in front of buildings in version 2.6, it has now been fundamentally upgraded to jump and pass smoothly over buildings and barriers without stopping.
  • Aircraft & Train Details (/) New in 3.4: While inside vehicles, pressing the Slash key (/) reads the exact aircraft altitude when flying, or reads the next and previous translated train stations when riding a train.

[5] Navigation System, Compass, and Difficulty Balance:

  • Knowing Your Targets: Using the Tracker menu (` / Backtick) or the Map (M), you can know the exact distance measured in steps, as well as the target's angle and direction (O).
  • Compass & Heading Updated in 3.4:
    • The compass reading key is now the Period key (.) instead of F.
    • You can now enter a heading angle numerically using the shortcut (Alt + .).
    • The rotation shortcuts (Alt + Z) and (Alt + X) now rotate the player by 45 degrees per press (clockwise or counter-clockwise).
  • Instant Teleport (Alt + P): Since object angles may not be 100% accurate under certain conditions, I added an instant teleport feature to reach the target.
  • Balancing Game Challenge:
    • On Foot: You can use Instant Teleport (Alt + P) to jump directly to the target.
    • Inside Vehicles: Instant teleport does not work inside a vehicle; instead, you must align your vehicle's angle to match the target's heading and drive forward until you reach your destination using the step counter and map, keeping the thrill and challenge of driving alive.

[6] Combat System, Experience (XP), and Destruction & Kill Tracking:

  • Auto-Aim (Alt + T): Locks onto the selected target or nearest threat, but does not fire on your behalf; instead, it executes your combat commands by delivering your weapon's damage to the target without requiring you to visually aim at them.
  • Experience (XP) & Coin Rewards New in 3.4: When killing NPCs (via auto-aim or manually) or destroying vehicles, you earn XP and coins based on the target type (regular, armed, or police officer).
  • Combat & Destruction Stats (U) Updated in 3.4: For a kill to be counted in your stats, you must land the hits or fire the shots yourself. The (U) key has now been updated to report both the number of destroyed vehicles and your total kills.

[7] Comprehensive Shop, Integrated Inventory, Challenges & Leveling: New in 3.4

A complete gameplay and challenge system has been added, allowing you to enjoy the game without relying on cheats by fighting, earning coins, and purchasing items:

  • Comprehensive Shop (F4): Features 602 diverse items: 206 vehicles, 258 clothing items, 43 weapons, 25 ammo types, 48 tattoos, plus food and services (stored in your inventory as keys, cards, etc., with full buy, sell, and quantity selection support).
  • Integrated Inventory (I): A central bag combining weapons, ammo, health, armor, food, cheat items, and vehicle keys, allowing you to use, equip, unequip, or delete items.
  • Challenge & Item Consumption System: Items in the inventory are consumable; for example, vehicle keys can be used 5 times only and spawn the vehicle right beside you regardless of its type (even a boat on land!), while other items are consumed immediately upon use to increase excitement and challenge.
  • Level-Up Gifts: Every time you reach a new level, a "Sealed Gift Parcel" is delivered to your inventory accompanied by a special alert sound.
  • Automatic Data Saving: Everything in your inventory, coins, level, XP, and more is automatically saved between sessions.
    Note: Health, armor, and hunger are NOT saved automatically in order to keep gameplay challenging and exciting.

[8] Advanced Command Bar, Player Info, and Auto-Announcements: New in 3.4

  • Advanced Command Bar (F2): Pressing F2 opens a full command input bar to execute text commands directly (the command list is published with the Access package), with complete editing, selection, reading, and execution shortcuts. You can still open the classic Cheat Menu easily by typing /cheat in the command bar and pressing Enter.
  • Player Info Menu (L): Displays the "Player Status" section containing details about your coins, level, XP, health, armor, and more in one place.
  • Hunger Indicator (Y): A new feature to accurately read the player's current hunger level.
  • Automatic Pickup & Mission Announcements: Access automatically announces items you pick up in the world—such as money, weapons, ammo, armor, health, and collectibles—as well as mission status alerts (started, passed/succeeded, failed).

[9] The Truth About Story Missions:

For honesty and clarity: I have not been able to make the main story missions fully playable and completable for a blind player, because GTA missions rely entirely on complex, simultaneous visual reactions.

Illustrative Example: In the very first mission of the game (Sweet & Kendl), you must go to the player's house, get on a bicycle, and chase your friends through narrow alleys and sharp turns while a rival gang car attacks and shoots at you from behind. These intertwined visual complexities represent a massive challenge that I hope to find solutions for in the future.

However, Access currently announces mission status verbally (started, passed, failed), and work is underway to make side missions (such as Ambulance, Police, Trains, and Taxi) playable in upcoming updates.

[10] Comprehensive Audio Upgrade & Customizable Sound System:

Because the original game's audio is weak and mono-distributed:

  • I integrated files to enhance audio distribution into crisp Stereo, along with a realistic high-quality sound mod (HQ Dynamic Sounds) that makes footsteps, weapons, engines, and explosions clear and spatialized to make directional awareness easier.
  • Organized & Customizable Sound System New in 3.4: Access comes with a complete suite of sound effects for digits (0 to 9), typing, menus, the shop, inventory, teleportation, radar, and combat. You can also add and customize your own sounds by following the instructions in the file ضع_أصواتك_هنا.txt inside the sounds folder.
Technical Note: The sound mod modifies audio via direct memory injection, which in very rare cases may cause a sudden game crash, but its auditory benefit and high quality provide an indispensable experience.

[11] Installation Steps, Auto-Update, and Language Setup:

  1. Extract the downloaded archive using any extraction software (such as WinRAR or 7-Zip).
  2. Copy the access folder, the start_access launcher file, and all other Access-related files and folders included in the package.
  3. Paste all of these files into the main GTA San Andreas game directory.
  4. Very Important Technical Notice: Make sure the game's main executable file in the game folder is named in ALL UPPERCASE letters exactly like this: GTA_SA.exe so that Access and the Process Hook can recognize it successfully.
  5. Update Tip: It is recommended (as an optional, preferred step) to delete old log files inside the access folder such as nvda_plugin.log and speech.log so that a clean, fresh log is generated for your current session without piling onto previous logs.
  6. Launching & Auto-Updating Access New in 3.4: Launch the game using the start_access file. Upon running it, the auto-updater automatically checks the server before starting the game and fetches the latest version—say goodbye to manual downloading, copying, or forgetting updates.
Important Note Regarding Changing Access Language:
If you launch the game and find the Access language set to English (EN), or if you wish to change the language (for example, to Arabic):
  1. First, enter the game world.
  2. Press F9 to open the Settings menu.
  3. Navigate with the arrow keys until you reach the Language option.
  4. Press Enter or Space to change the option and select your desired language (e.g., ar or en).
  5. Press F9 or Escape to save settings and exit the menu.

[13] FAQ & Technical Troubleshooting:

Q1: The game asks for an older runtime package or shows an error related to DirectX / vorbisFile.dll?

Cause & Technical Solution: GTA: San Andreas relies on legacy DirectX 9.0c libraries and DirectPlay features. Modern Windows systems (such as Windows 10 and Windows 11) include DirectX 12 only without legacy D3D9 files. To fix this:

  1. Ensure you replaced the original vorbisFile.dll with the one included in the Access/game package (ASI Loader).
  2. Ensure DirectPlay is enabled via: Control Panel → Programs and Features → Turn Windows features on or off → Legacy Components → DirectPlay.
  3. If the issue persists, install the official Microsoft DirectX End-User Runtimes (June 2010) package (link provided in the Links section below).
Q2: The game won't launch at all or closes immediately upon starting the game world (Crash on Startup)?

Cause & Solution: Some cracked or modified versions of GTA conflict directly with the ModLoader mod injector. Solution: Go to the main game directory, delete (or move) the file modloader.asi, and relaunch the game.

Q3: Sudden crash and exit from the game while roaming the world and playing?

Cause & Solution: This sudden crash is usually caused by the 3D dynamic sound pack and audio memory injection. Solution: Delete the INSANITY folder inside the modloader folder, or delete modloader.asi.

Q4: Experiencing lag or slow response from the NVDA screen reader during heavy combat and crowds?

Technical Reason: This behavior is normal and technically expected; Access scans memory arrays in real time and sends rapid, intensive text streams to nvdaControllerClient32.dll. When a huge number of pedestrians, vehicles, and gunshots occur simultaneously, memory calls queue up, causing slight momentary slowdown.

Q5: Game audio or radio stops working after teleporting on the map?

Cause & Solution: This happens because new map sectors load faster than the audio engine's streaming speed. Simple fix: Press Escape to open the game's Pause Menu, then press Escape again to Resume, and all audio will immediately return to normal.

Q6: How can I diagnose crashes and trace software bugs myself?

Solution: The access folder contains a primary diagnostic log file named nvda_plugin.log. This file records memory injection details, game state, and screen reader calls moment by moment; whenever you encounter an unusual issue, open this file to inspect the exact cause of the error.
